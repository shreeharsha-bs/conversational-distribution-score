Conversational Distribution Score

CDS compares a distribution of conversational behaviour with high-success human conversations (HSC) and a second anchor. We use either medium-success human conversations (MSC) or synthetic dialogues from Behaviour-SD as the second anchor.

CDS = 100 ×distance to second anchordistance to HSC + distance to second anchor

Distances are weighted empirical Wasserstein-2 distances. A higher CDS means more HSC-like behaviour relative to the second anchor. A score of 50 means equal distances, which can both be large or small
